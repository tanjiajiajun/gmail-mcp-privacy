Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This personal application connects the owner's Gmail account to Hermes MCP.

Data accessed

The application may access Gmail data required for enabled features, including email messages and the ability to create drafts. It requests only the Google permissions needed for those features.

How data is used

Gmail data is used only to provide requested personal productivity functions through Hermes. Gmail data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Data storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally by Hermes. Gmail content may be processed by Hermes and its configured AI service when needed to answer a user request. Gmail data is not intentionally shared with other third parties.

Data retention

The user can revoke access at any time from Google Account security settings. Locally stored OAuth credentials can also be deleted from the Hermes installation.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the account owner through the Google account associated with this integration.
